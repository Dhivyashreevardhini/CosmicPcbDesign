01 COSMIC PCB ENGINEERING

Established in 2014, COSMIC is a Coimbatore-based PCB design and manufacturing company serving customers across a range of electronic applications.

With experience in PCB designing, single-sided PCBs, double-sided PCBs, multilayer PCBs and PCB assembly, COSMIC aims to provide customers with a dependable manufacturing partner from design through production.

Our goal is simple: deliver PCB solutions that meet our customers' technical requirements while maintaining consistent quality and dependable service.
